# WaKiL Complaint Categories / Kategori Aduan

Each section below is one category. Use it to classify a complaint and suggest who should receive it.
Routing is a default suggestion; the citizen can change it before confirming.

---

## Category: Infrastructure/Roads (Infrastruktur / Jalan)

**Default routing:** ADUN (add MP if it is a federal highway).

**Covers:** damaged or potholed roads, broken or missing streetlights, damaged bridges, faulty traffic lights, broken pedestrian walkways, missing road signs, damaged public facilities (bus stops, playgrounds, community halls, public toilets).

**Examples:**
- "There is a big pothole on Jalan Mawar, a motorcycle fell yesterday."
- "Lampu jalan di Taman Seri sudah rosak dua minggu."
- "The pedestrian bridge near the school has a broken railing."

**Keywords:** pothole, lubang jalan, jalan rosak, streetlight, lampu jalan, bridge, jambatan, traffic light, lampu isyarat, road sign, bus stop, perhentian bas.

**Not this category:** flooding or blocked drains (Drainage & Flood), crime or accidents in progress (Public Safety).

**Priority guide:**
- High: danger to life now (collapsed bridge, huge pothole on a fast road, traffic lights down at a busy junction).
- Medium: damage that affects daily use (potholes on side roads, several streetlights out).
- Low: cosmetic or minor wear (faded markings, one dim streetlight).

**Note:** Federal highways and expressways are federal matters, so the citizen may want to add their MP.

## Category: Drainage & Flood (Saliran & Banjir)

**Default routing:** ADUN.

**Covers:** blocked or overflowing drains, flash floods, recurring flooding, broken drain covers, clogged culverts, river or monsoon drain overflow near homes, poor drainage after construction.

**Examples:**
- "The road floods every time it rains for more than an hour."
- "Longkang di depan rumah tersumbat dan berbau busuk."
- "A drain cover is broken near the school gate."

**Keywords:** flood, banjir, drain, longkang, parit, blocked, tersumbat, overflow, melimpah, culvert, flash flood, banjir kilat.

**Not this category:** rubbish in drains as a cleanliness issue only (Waste & Sanitation). If the main issue is flooding, keep it here.

**Priority guide:**
- High: water entering homes, people trapped, strong current, flooding that blocks emergency access.
- Medium: roads or yards flooded repeatedly, drains blocked with standing water.
- Low: small blockage without flooding, slow drainage.

**Note:** Local drainage is usually maintained by the local council (PBT), which is state-supervised.

## Category: Waste & Sanitation (Sisa & Kebersihan)

**Default routing:** ADUN.

**Covers:** uncollected rubbish, missed garbage collection, illegal dumping, overflowing public bins, dirty public toilets, overgrown grass or vacant lots, pest breeding sites, bad smell from waste sites.

**Examples:**
- "Rubbish has not been collected for two weeks."
- "Ada orang buang sampah haram di tanah kosong belakang rumah."
- "The public toilet at the market is very dirty."

**Keywords:** rubbish, sampah, garbage, collection, kutipan sampah, illegal dumping, buang sampah haram, bin, tong sampah, toilet awam, overgrown, semak, mosquito, nyamuk.

**Not this category:** blocked drains causing floods (Drainage & Flood).

**Priority guide:**
- High: health hazard (dead animals, large illegal dump near homes, dengue breeding site with active cases).
- Medium: collection missed for more than a week, overflowing bins.
- Low: litter, small overgrown patches.

**Note:** Waste collection is usually handled by the local council (PBT) or its appointed contractor.

## Category: Public Safety (Keselamatan Awam)

**Default routing:** MP (the citizen may also add ADUN).

**Covers:** crime concerns (theft, break-ins, vandalism trends), unsafe areas, drug activity concerns, lack of police patrols, illegal gambling or racing in the neighbourhood, dangerous structures or abandoned buildings, child or community safety concerns.

**Examples:**
- "There have been three break-ins in our taman this month."
- "Mat rempit berlumba di jalan depan rumah setiap malam."
- "An abandoned building is being used by drug users."

**Keywords:** crime, jenayah, theft, curi, break-in, pecah rumah, police, polis, PDRM, patrol, ronda, drugs, dadah, vandalism, unsafe, tidak selamat.

**Not this category:** damaged roads or lights (Infrastructure/Roads), even when the user says it feels unsafe.

**Priority guide:**
- High: repeated crimes, threats to residents, danger to children.
- Medium: ongoing nuisance or suspicious activity.
- Low: general request for more patrols.

**IMPORTANT:** WaKiL is not an emergency service. For a crime in progress, fire, or injury, call **999** first. Do not wait for a complaint ticket.

**Note:** Policing (PDRM) is a federal matter.

## Category: Utilities (Utiliti: Air & Elektrik)

**Default routing:** Both MP and ADUN.

**Covers:** water supply cuts, low water pressure, dirty or discoloured water, burst pipes, electricity outages, faulty transformers, fallen power lines, unsafe cables, high-voltage hazards.

**Examples:**
- "We have had no water for three days."
- "Bekalan elektrik sering terputus di kawasan kami."
- "The water from the tap is brown."

**Keywords:** water, air, no water, air tiada, pipe, paip pecah, electricity, elektrik, blackout, power cut, bekalan terputus, transformer, TNB, SESB, SEB, cable, kabel.

**Not this category:** billing disputes with the utility company (the citizen should contact the provider first; Others if they still want to report).

**Priority guide:**
- High: fallen live wires, no water for more than a day for many households, outage affecting clinics or elderly or disabled residents.
- Medium: repeated short outages, low pressure for several days.
- Low: single brief outage, minor leak with no service loss.

**Note:** Water is mostly state-linked and electricity is mostly federally-linked, so complaints default to both.

---

## Category: Welfare & Aid (Kebajikan & Bantuan)

**Default routing:** MP (the citizen may choose ADUN instead).

**Covers:** difficulty applying for or receiving government aid, welfare programmes, help for the elderly, disabled, single parents, or low-income families, rejected aid applications, missing assistance payments, requests for guidance on eligibility.

**Examples:**
- "My aid application was rejected and I do not know why."
- "Saya orang kurang upaya dan tidak tahu cara mohon bantuan."
- "An elderly neighbour lives alone and has no support."

**Keywords:** aid, bantuan, welfare, kebajikan, JKM, e-Kasih, subsidy, subsidi, disabled, OKU, elderly, warga emas, single mother, ibu tunggal, B40, poor, miskin.

**Not this category:** emergencies or abuse in progress (call 999 or the relevant helpline).

**Priority guide:**
- High: person in immediate hardship (no food, no shelter, at-risk elderly or child).
- Medium: stalled or rejected application affecting income.
- Low: general question about eligibility.

**Note:** Many programmes (for example through JKM and e-Kasih) are federal, but some states run their own schemes.

---

## Category: Others (Lain-lain)

**Default routing:** Both MP and ADUN, flagged for manual review.

**Use when:** the complaint does not fit any category above, the description is too vague to classify, or classification confidence is low.

**Examples:**
- A mix of unrelated issues in one message.
- A complaint with no clear subject or location.
- Issues outside wakil rakyat scope (for example private disputes).

**What happens:** staff manually re-categorise the ticket before triage, so it is not lost.

**Priority guide:** default Medium until a reviewer decides.
