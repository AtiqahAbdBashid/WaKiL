# WaKiL FAQ / Soalan Lazim

Each entry is self-contained. Answers are in English with a short Bahasa Malaysia version.

---

## What is WaKiL? / Apa itu WaKiL?

**Answer:** WaKiL is a platform where citizens report local problems to their wakil rakyat (elected representative) through a chatbot, then track progress until it is resolved.

**Jawapan:** WaKiL ialah platform untuk rakyat melaporkan masalah setempat kepada wakil rakyat melalui chatbot dan menjejak kemajuan sehingga selesai.

---

## What is a wakil rakyat, MP and ADUN? / Apakah wakil rakyat, MP dan ADUN?

**Answer:** A wakil rakyat is an elected representative. An MP (Ahli Parlimen) represents your parliamentary constituency in Parliament and handles federal matters. An ADUN (Ahli Dewan Undangan Negeri) represents your state constituency in the state assembly and handles state and local matters. Every citizen has one MP and one ADUN based on their address.

**Jawapan:** Wakil rakyat ialah wakil yang dipilih. MP mewakili kawasan Parlimen anda (urusan persekutuan). ADUN mewakili kawasan DUN anda (urusan negeri dan tempatan). Setiap rakyat mempunyai seorang MP dan seorang ADUN berdasarkan alamat.

---

## Who should I report to, MP or ADUN? / Patutkah saya lapor kepada MP atau ADUN?

**Answer:** WaKiL suggests a default based on the category. Roads, drains and rubbish usually go to your ADUN. Policing and welfare aid usually go to your MP. Water and electricity go to both. You can change the suggestion before you confirm.

**Jawapan:** WaKiL mencadangkan pilihan lalai mengikut kategori. Jalan, longkang dan sampah biasanya kepada ADUN. Polis dan bantuan kebajikan biasanya kepada MP. Air dan elektrik kepada kedua-duanya. Anda boleh ubah sebelum mengesahkan.

---

## What can I complain about? / Apa yang boleh saya adukan?

**Answer:** Local and community problems: damaged roads and streetlights, floods and blocked drains, rubbish and sanitation, public safety concerns, water and electricity issues, and welfare or aid problems. If your issue does not fit, it is filed under Others and a staff member reviews it.

**Jawapan:** Masalah setempat dan komuniti: jalan dan lampu jalan rosak, banjir dan longkang tersumbat, sampah dan kebersihan, keselamatan awam, isu air dan elektrik, serta bantuan kebajikan. Jika tidak sesuai, aduan dimasukkan ke Lain-lain dan disemak oleh kakitangan.

---

## What can I NOT complain about? / Apa yang tidak boleh diadukan?

**Answer:** Private disputes (for example with a neighbour or landlord), legal cases, and matters that are not the responsibility of an elected representative. WaKiL also cannot handle emergencies.

**Jawapan:** Pertikaian peribadi (contohnya dengan jiran atau tuan rumah), kes undang-undang, dan perkara yang bukan tanggungjawab wakil rakyat. WaKiL juga tidak boleh mengendalikan kecemasan.

---

## What if it is an emergency? / Bagaimana jika ia kecemasan?

**Answer:** Call **999** immediately for a crime in progress, fire, serious injury, or danger to life. WaKiL is not an emergency service, and filing a ticket will not bring an immediate response.

**Jawapan:** Hubungi **999** segera untuk jenayah sedang berlaku, kebakaran, kecederaan serius, atau bahaya kepada nyawa. WaKiL bukan perkhidmatan kecemasan.

---

## How do I file a complaint? / Bagaimana saya membuat aduan?

**Answer:** Log in, open the chatbot, and describe the problem. The chatbot asks a few simple questions: what happened, where, when, and whether you have a photo. It then shows a summary. Check it, edit if needed, and confirm to create your ticket.

**Jawapan:** Log masuk, buka chatbot dan terangkan masalah. Chatbot akan bertanya beberapa soalan mudah: apa berlaku, di mana, bila, dan sama ada ada gambar. Kemudian ia menunjukkan ringkasan. Semak, ubah jika perlu, dan sahkan untuk mencipta tiket.

---

## What information do I need to prepare? / Apa maklumat yang perlu disediakan?

**Answer:** What happened, where it is (street, taman, or landmark), and when it happened or when you first noticed it. A photo is helpful but optional. You can still file without one.

**Jawapan:** Apa yang berlaku, lokasi (jalan, taman atau mercu tanda), dan bila ia berlaku atau bila anda mula perasan. Gambar membantu tetapi tidak wajib.

---

## I do not know exactly when it happened. Can I still file? / Saya tidak pasti bila ia berlaku. Boleh saya teruskan?

**Answer:** Yes. Tell the chatbot when you first noticed the problem, for example "yesterday morning" or "since last week".

**Jawapan:** Boleh. Beritahu chatbot bila anda mula perasan masalah itu, contohnya "semalam pagi" atau "sejak minggu lepas".

---

## Do I need to choose the priority or category myself? / Perlukah saya pilih keutamaan atau kategori?

**Answer:** No. The AI suggests the category and priority from your description. You can see them on the summary screen before you confirm.

**Jawapan:** Tidak. AI mencadangkan kategori dan keutamaan berdasarkan penerangan anda. Anda boleh melihatnya pada skrin ringkasan sebelum mengesahkan.

---

## Which languages can I use? / Bahasa apa yang boleh digunakan?

**Answer:** English and Bahasa Malaysia. The chatbot replies in the language you use. If you write in another language, it replies in English.

**Jawapan:** Bahasa Inggeris dan Bahasa Malaysia. Chatbot menjawab dalam bahasa yang anda gunakan. Jika anda menulis dalam bahasa lain, ia menjawab dalam Bahasa Inggeris.

---

## What happens after I confirm? / Apa berlaku selepas saya mengesahkan?

**Answer:** You receive a unique ticket ID. Your complaint goes to the suggested wakil rakyat. You can follow its status in My Complaints: Pending, In Progress, Resolved, or Rejected, with a timeline of updates.

**Jawapan:** Anda menerima ID tiket unik. Aduan dihantar kepada wakil rakyat yang dicadangkan. Anda boleh menjejak status dalam Aduan Saya: Pending, In Progress, Resolved atau Rejected, dengan garis masa kemas kini.

---

## How long will it take to be resolved? / Berapa lama ia akan diselesaikan?

**Answer:** WaKiL cannot promise a resolution time. It depends on the issue, the responsible agency, and the representative's office. You can follow progress and updates on your ticket timeline.

**Jawapan:** WaKiL tidak boleh menjanjikan masa penyelesaian. Ia bergantung pada isu, agensi bertanggungjawab dan pejabat wakil rakyat. Anda boleh menjejak kemajuan pada garis masa tiket.

---

## What do the ticket statuses mean? / Apakah maksud status tiket?

**Answer:** Pending: submitted and waiting to be reviewed. In Progress: accepted, and the representative's team is working on it. Resolved: completed, usually with a job-done update. Rejected: not actionable, with a reason shown on the timeline.

**Jawapan:** Pending: dihantar dan menunggu semakan. In Progress: diterima dan sedang diusahakan. Resolved: selesai, biasanya dengan kemas kini kerja siap. Rejected: tidak boleh diambil tindakan, dengan sebab dipaparkan pada garis masa.

---

## Why was my complaint rejected? / Mengapa aduan saya ditolak?

**Answer:** A rejection always includes a reason from the representative's office, shown on your ticket timeline. Common reasons are that the matter is outside their responsibility, the details are unclear, or it duplicates another ticket. You can file a new, clearer complaint.

**Jawapan:** Penolakan sentiasa disertakan sebab daripada pejabat wakil rakyat, dipaparkan pada garis masa tiket. Sebab biasa: di luar bidang kuasa, butiran tidak jelas, atau pendua. Anda boleh membuat aduan baru yang lebih jelas.

---

## What if someone already reported the same problem? / Bagaimana jika orang lain sudah melaporkan masalah sama?

**Answer:** WaKiL checks for similar complaints in your area and category. If there is a match, your report is linked to it and counts as another resident reporting the same issue. This helps the representative see how many people are affected.

**Jawapan:** WaKiL menyemak aduan serupa di kawasan dan kategori anda. Jika ada, laporan anda dipautkan dan dikira sebagai penduduk lain yang melaporkan isu sama. Ini membantu wakil rakyat melihat berapa ramai terjejas.

---

## Can I report to a wakil rakyat outside my area? / Boleh saya lapor kepada wakil rakyat luar kawasan saya?

**Answer:** You can browse any representative's public dashboard, but you can only file complaints to your own MP and ADUN, based on your verified address.

**Jawapan:** Anda boleh melihat papan pemuka awam mana-mana wakil rakyat, tetapi hanya boleh membuat aduan kepada MP dan ADUN kawasan anda, berdasarkan alamat yang disahkan.

---

## Why is my address verified? / Mengapa alamat saya disahkan?

**Answer:** Your address decides which parliamentary and state constituency you belong to, so your complaint reaches the correct MP and ADUN. If verification fails, you can re-enter your address or contact support.

**Jawapan:** Alamat menentukan kawasan Parlimen dan DUN anda supaya aduan sampai kepada MP dan ADUN yang betul. Jika pengesahan gagal, anda boleh masukkan semula alamat atau hubungi sokongan.

---

## What if I leave the chat halfway? / Bagaimana jika saya tinggalkan chat separuh jalan?

**Answer:** You can keep it as a draft and continue later, or discard it. No ticket is created until you confirm the summary.

**Jawapan:** Anda boleh simpan sebagai draf dan sambung kemudian, atau buang. Tiket tidak dicipta sehingga anda mengesahkan ringkasan.

---

## Is my information private? / Adakah maklumat saya peribadi?

**Answer:** Your complaint is visible to you and to the wakil rakyat it is assigned to. Your personal details are used only to verify your constituency and handle your complaint. Do not share passwords or bank details in the chat.

**Jawapan:** Aduan anda hanya boleh dilihat oleh anda dan wakil rakyat yang ditugaskan. Maklumat peribadi digunakan hanya untuk mengesahkan kawasan dan mengendalikan aduan. Jangan kongsi kata laluan atau butiran bank dalam chat.

---

## Does the chatbot make final decisions? / Adakah chatbot membuat keputusan akhir?

**Answer:** No. The AI collects details and suggests a category and priority. You confirm the summary, and staff can re-categorise unclear tickets. The representative's office decides how to act.

**Jawapan:** Tidak. AI mengumpul butiran dan mencadangkan kategori serta keutamaan. Anda mengesahkan ringkasan, dan kakitangan boleh mengkategori semula tiket yang tidak jelas. Pejabat wakil rakyat memutuskan tindakan.
